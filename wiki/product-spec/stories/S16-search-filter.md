# S16: Search and filter shots

Status: specced (owner approved 2026-11-10: "finish 10-20")

Epic: [E2 the workbench](E2-workbench.md). Source: [workbench UX plan](../plans/workbench-ux.md), section 10, 2026-11-07.

## Story

A person finds shots by text, status or unresolved stickies on a large project.

## Acceptance

1. A search field filters the graph by title, action, dialogue and notes; non-matches dim.
2. Filters for status and has-unresolved-sticky combine with search.
3. Results respond within 60 ms on the 30-scene fixture.
